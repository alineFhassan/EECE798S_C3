FiberLink Consultancy is a Lebanese engineering consultancy that specializes in supervising and providing technical consultation for fiber optic installation projects. Our mission is to accelerate Lebanon’s digital transformation by ensuring high-quality, reliable, and efficient fiber optic networks built to international standards.

We work closely with contractors, telecom operators, and government agencies to oversee every stage of network deployment. Our role covers project supervision, design verification, field coordination, and quality control. We make sure every installation is executed safely, efficiently, and in full compliance with national and international standards.

Over the past years, FiberLink Consultancy has successfully supervised several major fiber optic expansion projects across Lebanon. These include network rollout supervision in Beirut and Mount Lebanon, quality assurance for municipal fiber extensions in the North, and technical consulting for private sector infrastructure integration. Our team has also supported the rehabilitation of existing telecom lines and the implementation of underground and aerial fiber systems in coordination with key contractors.

Led by Aline Hassan (CEO), Diana Hassan (Project Manager), and Mohammad Hassan (Senior Project Manager), our team includes experienced engineers such as Aya Malak, Rayan Saad, Taha Hassan, Mostafa Merhi, and Lynn Hachem. Together, we bring expertise in civil, telecom, and surveying engineering to every project, ensuring technical excellence and transparent communication.

What makes FiberLink unique is our ability to combine local field knowledge with international engineering standards. We believe in precision, transparency, and collaboration, values that guide our partnerships with contractors and clients alike. Our goal is to help Lebanon build a future-ready, high-speed communication network that connects communities and powers economic growth.

Looking ahead, FiberLink Consultancy aims to expand its services across the Middle East by providing sustainable fiber network solutions and next-generation telecom infrastructure consultation.
